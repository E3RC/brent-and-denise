Why We're Leaving America

Brent & Denise · July 26, 2026

The decision built up slowly. For years we watched grocery bills climb, property taxes rise, and our retirement accounts lag behind inflation. One evening we sat down with the spreadsheet we had opened a hundred times before and asked a different question: what if we didn't stay?

That question took us to countries where our savings stretch further, where healthcare is affordable, where a couple can live well on what we have built over forty years. The more we read, the more obvious the answer looked.

We know it surprises people. Our kids needed time to get used to it. Friends have called us brave and crazy in the same conversation.

Once you see a path to a retirement where money is not the first thing on your mind every morning, and there is room for the adventure we have been putting off, it is hard to stop seeing it. This blog is how we will document the move, share what we learn, and answer the question other people our age are already asking.

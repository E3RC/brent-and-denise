Our Retirement Timeline: The Countdown Begins

Brent & Denise · July 30, 2026

Denise retires in December 2027. She has given HR the date. After thirty-two years in public education she is ready to trade lesson plans for travel plans, and her pension vests fully at the end of that calendar year. She is giving the school maximum notice, but that last day is circled in red on our kitchen calendar.

My timeline runs later. I turn sixty in 2031, and my Social Security benefit nearly doubles if I wait for full retirement age. So Denise moves first, once we settle on a country, and I follow when the consulting work winds down. We will do long distance for a while and visit every six to eight weeks. Run the numbers and the staggered approach adds about twenty-three hundred dollars a month to our combined benefits, which matters on a fixed income.

Between now and her last day we are in preparation mode: downsizing the house, selling the second car, sorting through thirty years of accumulated stuff. The goal is to cut our monthly overhead by at least forty percent before she gets on a plane. Every dollar we cut now is a dollar we do not have to worry about later. The countdown feels more exciting than scary.

Why We're Leaving America

We didn't decide this in a weekend. We've spent years talking through what our future looks like, and every round of planning pointed the same way: the life we want is somewhere else.

Housing, groceries, insurance and utilities take a bigger share of our income every year, even with steady jobs and a budget we stick to. We've re-run the retirement projections each year, and they keep landing short of what we hoped.

Healthcare is the part we can't plan around. We've seen other people's retirement plans come apart over medical costs, and the uncertainty grows as we get older. Countries with universal coverage offer a stability we can't find here.

Beyond the numbers, we want a simpler life: less stress, more time outside, closer ties with each other and with whatever community we join. We want to wake up thinking about the day ahead instead of the next bill.

Resources for Your Own Journey

Here are the tools, websites, and services we've found helpful in planning our retirement and move abroad.

Retirement Planning

  • Social Security Administration — benefits calculator and official info
  • AARP Retirement Planning — guides, tools, and articles for retirement
  • FIRE Calculator — retirement withdrawal simulator

Moving Abroad

  • Expat Exchange — expat community and relocation resources
  • Internations — global expat network and guides
  • Visa Information by Country — U.S. State Department travel visa guidance

Cost of Living

  • Numbeo — crowd-sourced cost of living data worldwide
  • Expatistan — compare cost of living between cities

Healthcare

  • Cigna Global — international health insurance plans
  • CDC Travel Health Info — destinations, vaccines, and health advisories

Why We're Leaving America

This isn't a decision we made lightly. We've spent years talking through what our future looks like, and the more we planned, the clearer it became that the life we want is somewhere else. It's not about running away — it's about running toward something better.

The rising cost of living has made it harder to save at the pace we need. Even with solid jobs and careful budgeting, housing, groceries, insurance, and utilities keep eating up more of our income. We've watched our retirement projections shift year after year, and the math just doesn't add up the way it used to.

Healthcare is the biggest unknown. We've seen too many retirement plans derailed by medical costs, and the older we get, the more that uncertainty weighs on us. Countries with universal healthcare systems offer a stability we can't find here — and that peace of mind is worth more than we can put into words.

But beyond the numbers, we want a simpler life. Less stress, more time outside, deeper connections with each other and the communities we join. We want to wake up excited about the day ahead, not worried about the next bill. That's the future we're building, one step at a time.

Resources for Your Own Journey

Here are the tools, websites, and services we've found helpful in planning our retirement and move abroad.

Retirement Planning

  • Social Security Administration — benefits calculator and official info
  • AARP Retirement Planning — guides, tools, and articles for retirement
  • FIRE Calculator — retirement withdrawal simulator

Moving Abroad

  • Expat Exchange — expat community and relocation resources
  • Internations — global expat network and guides
  • Visa Information by Country — U.S. State Department travel visa guidance

Cost of Living

  • Numbeo — crowd-sourced cost of living data worldwide
  • Expatistan — compare cost of living between cities

Healthcare

  • Cigna Global — international health insurance plans
  • CDC Travel Health Info — destinations, vaccines, and health advisories
